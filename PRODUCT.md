# Product

## Register

Sooca Coffee is a B2B green coffee supplier specializing in high-quality, traceable Indonesian specialty beans. The website serves as a digital storefront and trust-builder for international coffee roasters, importers, and traders looking for a reliable origin partner with authentic supply chain transparency and consistent product quality.

## Users

**Target Audience:** B2B buyers — specialty coffee roasters, importers, and traders sourcing unroasted green beans globally.

**Characteristics:** Procurement-minded professionals who are skeptical of marketing claims, knowledgeable about coffee, and actively comparing multiple suppliers. They have high qualification standards and are evaluating both product quality and supplier reliability.

**Website Goal:** The site must quickly establish Sooca Coffee as a credible, traceable, and professional origin partner. Visitors should feel confident enough to initiate contact through a inquiry form or direct outreach. The site removes friction from the qualification process by providing the specificity and transparency they're already seeking.

## Product Purpose

A focused one-page website that establishes Sooca Coffee as a serious, traceable Indonesian green coffee supplier. The site communicates:
- **Product quality** through origin stories, processing details, and bean varietals
- **Supply chain transparency** with verifiable sourcing, certifications, and farmer relationships
- **Reliability** through consistent supply, professional operations, and clear communication

**Success Metric:** Qualified B2B buyers submit contact inquiries or reach out directly by phone/email, indicating confidence in Sooca Coffee as a potential supplier worth engaging with.

## Brand Personality

**Tone:** Calm, authoritative, grounded.

**Visual Feel:** Professional yet approachable. The site should feel like speaking with an experienced supplier who knows their craft and business, not a marketing-heavy startup or a lifestyle cafe. Every design choice signals confidence and competence.

**How it works on the website:**
- Copy is direct and specific—no buzzwords or vague claims
- Imagery focuses on real places, processes, and people (not styled lifestyle shots)
- Layout is clean and organized, letting content breathe
- Typography and spacing communicate respect for the reader's time

## Anti-references

The website should **avoid** these approaches:

- **Generic cafe branding** (Starbucks-style warmth, consumer-facing coffee shop aesthetics) — this is not a retail experience; it signals amateur-hour to business buyers
- **SaaS startup landing pages** (metric grids, feature-bullet sections, gradient CTAs, hero stats) — this is not innovation software; we're an origin supplier, not a tech company
- **Hipster third-wave over-artiness** (moody filters, artisan theater, lifestyle romanticization) — this alienates professional buyers who want facts, not Instagram vibes
- **Generic cold B2B corporate sites** (navy blue, clip-art diagrams, "synergy" copy) — we're not a faceless corporation; the site should feel human without being soft

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through specificity** — Use concrete details (origin village names, actual drying processes, real farmer names) over vague marketing claims. Every product description should include specific tasting notes, processing methods, and harvest timing.

2. **Restraint signals quality** — A sparse, deliberate layout reads as confidence; busy layouts read as desperation. Whitespace, clear hierarchy, and limited color palette communicate professionalism and respect for the visitor's time.

3. **The buyer is skeptical** — Every section should answer an unspoken objection ("How do I know this is real?" "Can I trust your supply chain?" "Are these beans actually good?"), not celebrate the brand. Anticipate questions and answer them directly.

4. **Contact is the conversion** — Every page section should make reaching out feel like a natural next step, not a forced call-to-action. Make contact pathways visible and easy without being pushy.

5. **Indonesia is the product** — The origin story is the differentiation. Lean into the geography, culture, and terroir visually and in copy. Real photographs of fields, processing facilities, and people build authenticity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sooca Coffee serves international buyers who may have different abilities, devices, or connectivity speeds. Accessibility isn't an add-on—it's a sign of respect and professionalism.

**Standards & Requirements:**
- **WCAG AA minimum** across all pages — meets legal standards and best practices
- **Keyboard navigable** — users should be able to access all content and functions via keyboard alone
- **Reduced-motion respected** — animations and auto-play should respect user preferences for motion sensitivity
- **No reliance on color alone** — information conveyed through color should also be conveyed through text, icons, or contrast

**Business Benefit:** A buyer using a screen reader or on a slow mobile connection in a remote location is still a qualified lead. Removing barriers expands our addressable market.
